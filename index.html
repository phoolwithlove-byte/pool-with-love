Our Story

Why we bend wire instead of picking petals

Phool with Love began with a simple question: what if a bouquet could last as long as the feeling behind it? Fresh flowers are beautiful for a week — we wanted to make something that stays beautiful for years.

Every bouquet is hand-shaped from premium pipe cleaners and plush fabric, stem by stem, in our small HSR Layout studio. No two are ever quite identical — and that's exactly the point.
